- operational instability,
- fulfillment bottlenecks,
- conversion friction,
- customer retention risks,
- inventory inefficiencies,
- pricing pressure,
- promotional dependency,
- financial performance risks.

1. operational reasoning,
2. customer experience implications,
3. fulfillment reliability,
4. revenue sustainability,
5. executive readability.

IMPORTANT:

- temporary promotional effects,
- structural operational issues,
- localized fulfillment instability,
- systemic customer experience degradation.

WRITING STYLE:
- Executive-level.
- Concise and operationally intelligent.
- Narrative-driven.
- Prioritized.
