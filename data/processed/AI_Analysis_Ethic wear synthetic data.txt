## 2. DATA PROFILING  | Column (as listed)                | What it Represents (in plain business terms)                              |
|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Date / Day**                    | Calendar timestamp and day‑of‑week – used for time‑series analysis.       |
| **Bills Issued**                  | Number of sales transactions (or invoices) generated on that day.       |
| **Footfall**                      | Total number of store visitors (traffic count).                           |
| **Qty**                           | Quantity of units sold (or units shipped).                                |
| **Sales**                         | Total sales revenue (currency) recorded for the day.                      |
| **Conversion Rate**               | Ratio of **Bills Issued** to **Footfall** (transactions ÷ visitors).     |
| **Average Transactional Value**   | Revenue per transaction ( Sales ÷ Bills Issued ).                        |
| **Average Selling Price**         | Average price of a unit sold ( Sales ÆQty ).                             |
| **Unit Per Transaction**          | Avg. number of units sold per transaction ( Qty ÷ Bills Issued ).        |

*Signature of the dataset*: a **daily snapshot** of a single retail outlet’s operational performance over a multi‑week period (the preview shows 15 consecutive days, spanning weekend and weekday traffic). The data is **synthetic** but mirrors realistic retail metrics, making it suitable for exploratory analysis, KPI definition, and modeling of sales drivers.



## 3. TOP 3 KPI’S A CEO SHOULD TRACK  | Rank | KPI (Column) | Why It Matters to a CEO | How It Is Calculated From the Data |
|------|--------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **1** | **Sales (Revenue)** | Direct measure of top‑line growth; drives profitability, cash‑flow, and strategic decisions (e.g., expansion, marketing spend). | Sum of the **Sales** column (total revenue) or break‑down by day/week/month to monitor trends. |
| **2** | **Conversion Rate** | Indicates how efficiently store traffic translates into sales; a critical lever for improving marketing ROI and store layout. | **Bills Issued** ÷ **Footfall** (expressed as a %). A rising rate signals better sales conversion; a declining rate flags operational or merchandising issues. |
| **3** | **Average Transactional Value (ATV)** | Reflects basket size and pricing power; higher ATV increases revenue without needing additional footfall. | **Sales** ÷ **Bills Issued** (or equivalently **Average Selling Price** may be used). Trends can guide promotions, upsell strategies, and pricing decisions. |

*Why these three?*  
- **Sales** captures the core financial outcome.  
- **Conversion Rate** ties **traffic** to **revenue**, highlighting operational efficiency.  - **ATV** uncovers the revenue contribution of each transaction, guiding staffing, inventory, and pricing tactics.  

Together they give a balanced view of top‑line performance, customer engagement, and monetisation of each sale.



## 4. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY  The file **`Ethic wear synthetic data.xlsx`** contains **15 daily rows** of transactional and foot‑traffic metrics from a single retail outlet that sells ethically‑sourced apparel.  Core fields include:

- **Date / Day** – temporal context (weekday vs weekend).  
- **Bills Issued** – number of completed sales transactions.  
- **Footfall** – total store visitors.  
- **Qty**, **Sales**, **Conversion Rate**, **Average Transactional Value**, **Average Selling Price**, **Unit Per Transaction** – derived performance indicators.

The dataset is structured as a typical **retail‑operations snapshot**, allowing analysis of revenue generation, customer conversion efficiency, and basket size.  It can be leveraged to:

- Identify high‑performing days (e.g., weekends with elevated footfall and sales).  
- Diagnose conversion inefficiencies (low Conversion Rate on certain weekdays).  
- Optimize pricing and promotional strategies through ATV and Average Selling Price trends.  

In short, the data provides a concise, daily view of sales effectiveness and customer traffic, enabling data‑driven decisions to boost profitability and operational efficiency.
