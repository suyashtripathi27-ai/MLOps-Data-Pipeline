### 🔍 4. Key Financial & Risk Findings

*   **Observation:** A significant 20% of the customer base has exited the bank.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with factors such as customer dissatisfaction, competitive offerings, or evolving financial needs not met by current products.
    *   **Business Impact:** This represents a direct loss of revenue streams, potential erosion of market share, and increased costs associated with customer acquisition to offset churn.

*   **Observation:** 25% of customers maintain a zero balance, despite the average customer balance being $76,485.89.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with dormant accounts, customers utilizing the bank for specific non-balance-holding products, or a primary banking relationship existing elsewhere.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may contribute to reduced overall liquidity, diminished interest income potential, and increased operational overhead for managing inactive accounts.

*   **Observation:** The average number of products held by customers is 1.53, with 75% of customers holding two or fewer products.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with limited cross-selling effectiveness, a lack of perceived value in additional product offerings, or insufficient product diversification strategies.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may contribute to lower customer lifetime value, reduced revenue per customer, and increased vulnerability to competitive pressures for single-product customers.

### 🚀 6. Recommended Actions

1.  **Risk Team:** Initiate a comprehensive analysis of the 20% customer churn rate, focusing on identifying common characteristics of exited customers (e.g., CreditScore, Age, Tenure, NumOfProducts) to inform targeted retention strategies.
2.  **Product Team:** Develop and implement targeted campaigns to increase product penetration, specifically for customers currently holding only one product, aiming to enhance customer stickiness and revenue diversification.
3.  **Branch Managers:** Implement proactive outreach programs for customers with zero balances or low activity, aiming to re-engage them and understand their financial needs to prevent further dormancy or churn.
