### 🔍 4. Key Financial & Risk Findings

*   **Observation:** A significant proportion of customer accounts (25th percentile) hold a zero balance.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with customers utilizing the bank for specific transactional services without maintaining primary deposit relationships, or potentially indicating dormant accounts requiring further investigation.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may contribute to reduced overall deposit liquidity, lower net interest income potential, and increased operational costs per customer if these accounts are not profitable.

*   **Observation:** The bank is experiencing a substantial customer churn rate, with 20% of customers having exited.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with factors such as customer dissatisfaction, competitive pressures, or evolving customer needs not being met by current product offerings.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may contribute to a decline in the customer base, reduced revenue streams, and increased acquisition costs to replace lost customers, impacting long-term financial stability.

*   **Observation:** The average number of products held by customers is 1.53, with 50% of customers holding only one product.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with limited cross-selling initiatives, a lack of perceived value in additional products, or a customer base primarily seeking single-product solutions.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may contribute to lower customer lifetime value, reduced opportunities for revenue diversification, and potentially weaker customer loyalty compared to multi-product relationships.

### 🚀 6. Recommended Actions

1.  **Product Team:** Initiate a targeted campaign to re-engage customers with zero or low balances, potentially offering incentives for increased deposit activity or introducing new product bundles designed to consolidate primary banking relationships.
2.  **Risk Team:** Conduct a deeper analysis into the 20% customer churn rate, segmenting exited customers by age, tenure, and product holdings to identify common patterns and potential root causes. This should inform retention strategies.
3.  **Branch Managers:** Implement focused training for customer-facing staff on identifying opportunities for cross-selling and upselling, particularly for customers currently holding only one product, to enhance customer lifetime value and product penetration.
