### 🔍 4. Key Financial & Risk Findings

*   **Observation:** 20% of the total customer base has exited the bank.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with various factors such as evolving customer needs, competitive offerings, or perceived value of banking services.
    *   **Business Impact:** This represents a substantial churn rate, which may contribute to revenue leakage and necessitate increased investment in customer acquisition efforts to maintain growth.

*   **Observation:** A significant portion of customers (25th percentile) maintain a zero balance, while the overall average customer balance is $76,485.89.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be linked to customers utilizing the bank for non-deposit services, holding dormant accounts, or having recently transferred funds out.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may indicate potential disengagement among a segment of the customer base, possibly impacting deposit stability and presenting a risk to overall liquidity management if not actively monitored.

*   **Observation:** The average number of products held by customers is 1.53, with 75% of customers holding two or fewer products.
    *   **Possible Reason:** This could be associated with limited cross-selling effectiveness or a customer base primarily engaging with a single core banking product.
    *   **Business Impact:** This may suggest missed opportunities for deepening customer relationships and increasing wallet share, potentially limiting revenue diversification and long-term customer value.

### 🚀 6. Recommended Actions

1.  **Risk Team & Product Team:** Initiate a comprehensive analysis of the 20% customer churn rate, focusing on identifying common attributes (e.g., CreditScore, Age, Tenure, NumOfProducts) of exited customers to inform targeted retention strategies and product enhancements.
2.  **Branch Managers & Product Team:** Develop and implement targeted engagement campaigns for customers with zero or low balances, exploring incentives for increased deposit activity or the introduction of relevant complementary products to foster deeper relationships.
3.  **Product Team & Marketing:** Review and optimize current product bundling and cross-selling frameworks to increase the average number of products per customer, leveraging insights from active member profiles to tailor offerings more effectively.
